Media Automations

Operated by Saranen Media

Media Automations is a private automation tool that helps Facebook and Instagram account owners manage interactions with people who comment on their posts.

What the service does

On connected Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts, the automation can detect comments containing designated keywords and initiate an automated response.

For example, when a person comments with a designated keyword such as “audio”, the automation can send a private message containing a button that allows the person to request the relevant resource. After the person requests the resource, the automation sends the requested link.

On Facebook, the automation can also post a public reply to the original comment informing the person that they have received a private message.

Facebook and Instagram

The service supports automated interactions on both Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts. The specific messaging and interaction capabilities available depend on the permissions and features provided by Meta for each platform.

Privacy and Data

Media Automations only processes information necessary to provide the automated interaction described above. Platform data received through Meta APIs is handled in accordance with our privacy practices.
